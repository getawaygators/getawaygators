Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Getaway Gators website and Getaway Gators Uploader (the "App"), a private tool that the operator of Getaway Gators uses to upload approved clips to the Getaway Gators accounts on YouTube and TikTok. The App is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

  • TikTok (via TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API): your basic account information (user.info.basic, such as an account identifier and display name) and permission to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload).
  • YouTube (via the YouTube Data API): permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • The access and refresh tokens these services issue so the App can keep uploading without asking you to sign in every time.

The only account that signs in to the App is the operator's own Getaway Gators account.

How the information is used

It is used only to upload videos that the operator has reviewed and approved, to the operator's own accounts. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is not sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator. They are not sent to any server other than TikTok's and Google's own sign-in and upload services.

Third-party services

The App talks to TikTok and to Google (YouTube). Their handling of your data is governed by their own policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service

The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and how to revoke access

Tokens are kept until they expire or access is revoked. You can revoke access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The App and this website are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
